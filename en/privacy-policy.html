Privacy policy

This is an English translation provided for convenience. If the two versions differ, the Czech version prevails.

This policy describes how I handle the personal data of website visitors and of people who contact me about individual basketball training.

Data controller

The controller of personal data is Alena Hanušová, Company ID (IČO) 07287607, contact email hanusova6@gmail.com.

Visitor analytics

The website uses Google Analytics 4 from Google Ireland Limited so I can see how many people find the website and which parts interest them. The tool stores cookies in your browser and processes technical data about the visit: approximate location at city level, device and browser type, where you came to the website from and which pages you viewed.

It won’t run without your consent. On your first visit a bar appears asking for it. Until you click “Accept”, not a single request goes to Google’s servers and no cookies are stored. The website works exactly the same without analytics.

  • Purpose: website traffic statistics.
  • Legal basis: your consent under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR.
  • Retention period: 14 months, after which Google deletes the data automatically.
  • Advertising features are turned off — the data is not used for ad targeting or remarketing.

As a processor, Google may transfer data to the United States. The transfer is covered by the European Commission’s adequacy decision (EU–US Data Privacy Framework).

You can withdraw your consent at any time using the “Visitor analytics settings” button in the footer of every page. Withdrawal does not affect processing that took place before it. You can also delete cookies in your browser settings.

What the website does not collect

Apart from the analytics described above, the website uses no advertising systems, has no forms and loads no content from third-party servers. Fonts are stored directly on the website, not on an external CDN.

When processing takes place

I process personal data only when you write to me yourself via WhatsApp or email. In that case it is the data you share with me in the email or message — typically your name, phone number or email address and information about the player, such as their age, club and what you want to work on.

Purpose and legal basis

  • Purpose: arranging and running training sessions, agreeing on the time and place, and invoicing where applicable.
  • Legal basis: performance of a contract under Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR; for communication before a session is arranged, legitimate interest under Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Retention period

I keep the data for the duration of our cooperation and for 3 years after it ends. Accounting and tax documents are kept for the period required by law, i.e. 10 years.

Who the data is shared with

I do not pass on data from your communication to anyone else or use it for marketing. The exception is the visitor analytics described above, where Google acts as the processor. If you contact me via WhatsApp, the communication takes place in an app operated by Meta Platforms, and its terms and privacy policy apply. Likewise, email communication passes through your email provider’s servers.

Your rights

In relation to your personal data you have the right:

  • to access the data I process about you,
  • to have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected,
  • to have data erased if there is no longer a reason to process it,
  • to restrict processing,
  • to object to processing based on legitimate interest,
  • to lodge a complaint with the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection (Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů), Pplk. Sochora 27, Prague 7.

You can exercise any of these rights by email at hanusova6@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If the way data is processed changes, I will update this page too. Last updated: 10 September 2026.
